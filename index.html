Baptiste Bohelay

Exploring Interactivity, Science and Sonic experiences since 2008.

Coming from a background in Science of Music, I have gravitated around various constellations: Video Games, Interactive Installations, Research in Music and Art. I kept one foot building my professional experience, one playing and collaborating with passionate artists.

I have been working with clients such as MTV, Heineken, Konami, the McGill University, Tangible Interaction, and contributed to various art projects sometimes big, mostly small, always fun and very human.

This website aims to reflect this exploration.
